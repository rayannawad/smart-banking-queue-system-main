CATEGORY 01

Booking a Queue

Everything you need to know before making a queue reservation.

How do I book a queue?

Open the “Book Your Turn” page, choose your bank, branch, required service, and preferred time. Review your details and confirm the booking to receive your queue reference.

Do I need an account to book?

No. QueueFlow allows customers to make a booking using basic contact information. Some banks may require additional verification for specific services.

Can I book more than one queue?

You can create multiple bookings when they are for different services or separate visits. Availability may depend on the selected bank.

How will I receive my queue number?

After confirming your reservation, QueueFlow displays your booking reference, queue number, selected branch, and estimated appointment time.

CATEGORY 02

Queue & Waiting

Understand how queue numbers and waiting estimates work.

How can I check my queue status?

Open the “Queue Status” page and enter your booking reference. You will see your current queue position and the latest estimated waiting time.

Is the waiting time always exact?

The waiting time is an estimate based on the current queue activity. It may change when a service takes longer or customers arrive early.

What happens if I arrive late?

Your booking may be marked as missed if you arrive after your queue number has been called. The branch may ask you to reserve a new queue number.

Can my queue position change?

Yes. Queue positions can change when bookings are canceled, customers do not arrive, or the bank updates its service availability.

CATEGORY 03

Manage a Booking

Update or cancel your booking before your scheduled visit.

How do I cancel my booking?

Open “Manage Booking,” enter your booking reference, and choose the cancellation option. A confirmation message will appear after the booking is canceled.

Can I change my appointment time?

If another time is available, you can update your appointment through the Manage Booking page before the booking deadline.

What is a booking reference?

A booking reference is a unique code created when your reservation is confirmed. It helps you view, update, or cancel your booking.

CATEGORY 04

Banks & Services

Learn more about supported banks, branches, and available services.

Which banks support QueueFlow?

You can visit the “Our Banks” page to see the complete list of supported banking partners and available branch locations.

Are all bank services available?

Available services may differ between banks and branches. After selecting a branch, QueueFlow displays the services currently supported at that location.

Can I book a service for someone else?

Some services may be booked for another person, but the bank may require the visitor to provide identification when arriving at the branch.

CATEGORY 05

Account & Privacy

Information about your personal details and data privacy.

How is my information used?

Your information is used to create and manage queue bookings, provide support, and improve the QueueFlow service experience.

Is my personal information secure?

QueueFlow is designed to protect customer information and limit access to authorized service purposes.

Where can I read the Privacy Policy?

You can review the complete Privacy Policy by selecting the privacy link available in the website footer.
