Your Data Is Important

We are committed to handling your information responsibly.

01

Introduction

Welcome to QueueFlow. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our smart banking queue services.

This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect, why we collect it, how it may be used, and the choices available to you regarding your data.

By using QueueFlow, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We Collect

We may collect information that helps us create and manage your queue reservation and provide a smooth service experience.

Information you provide

  • Full name and basic contact details.
  • Selected bank, branch, and service.
  • Preferred appointment date and time.
  • Booking details and queue references.

Information collected automatically

When you use the website, limited technical information may be collected, such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and general usage activity.

03

How We Use Your Information

QueueFlow uses collected information to provide and improve the services available through the platform.

Manage Bookings

Create, update, and confirm queue reservations.

Send Updates

Provide queue and appointment information.

Improve Services

Understand usage and improve the platform.

Provide Support

Respond to questions and service requests.

04

Information Sharing

QueueFlow does not sell personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary to provide a requested service or meet legal and operational requirements.

Limited booking information may be shared with the selected bank or branch to help process the queue reservation and prepare for the customer visit.

We Do Not Sell Your Data

Your personal information is not sold or rented to third parties for marketing.

05

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Although we work to protect your information, no online system can guarantee complete security. Users should also protect their booking references and personal details.

06

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to manage bookings, provide support, maintain service records, and meet applicable operational requirements.

Information that is no longer needed may be deleted, anonymized, or stored according to relevant data-management practices.

07

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the services available and applicable requirements, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

01

Access

Request information about the data connected to your account or booking.

02

Correction

Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.

03

Deletion

Request removal of information when it is no longer required.

08

Cookies & Website Technology

QueueFlow may use cookies and similar technologies to support website features, understand general usage, and improve the browsing experience.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work as expected if cookies are disabled.

09

Contact Us About Privacy

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request information related to your data, please contact our support team.
