### Home Page

The Home Page serves as the main entry point of the QueueFlow platform. It introduces the smart banking queue management system and highlights how customers can book banking services, manage their queue reservations, and reduce unnecessary waiting times.

#### Navigation Bar
The navigation bar provides access to the main sections of the platform, including Home, About, How It Works, Banks, Services, and Contact. It also includes a "Book Your Turn" button that provides direct access to the queue booking process.

#### Hero Section
The hero section introduces the platform with the message "Your Time Matters. Your Queue Should Too." It explains the main purpose of QueueFlow and provides two primary actions:
- **Book Your Turn:** Allows users to start booking a queue number.
- **How It Works:** Directs users to information explaining the booking process.

It also displays a sample current queue card containing the branch name, queue number, booking status, estimated waiting time, number of customers ahead, and queue progress percentage.

#### Platform Statistics
This section highlights key platform statistics, including:
- **10,000+ Active Users:** Represents the number of active users.
- **25+ Bank Branches:** Shows the number of participating bank branches.
- **40% Less Waiting Time:** Highlights the potential reduction in waiting time.
- **98% User Satisfaction:** Represents the user satisfaction rate.

#### Why QueueFlow?
This section explains the main benefits of the platform:
- **Book in Advance:** Allows customers to reserve their queue numbers before arriving at the bank.
- **Track Your Queue:** Enables users to monitor their queue status and see how many customers are ahead of them.
- **Stay Updated:** Provides updates to help customers know when their turn is approaching.
- **Built for Banks:** Helps banking institutions manage customer flow, reduce congestion, and improve branch operations.

#### Mobile App Preview
This section presents a preview of the mobile interface, showing a user's current queue number, the number of people ahead, and an option to view queue details.

#### How It Works
This section explains the queue booking process through three simple steps:
1. **Select Your Bank:** Choose the preferred bank and branch.
2. **Choose a Service:** Select the required banking service.
3. **Confirm Your Queue:** Confirm the reservation and receive a queue number with an estimated waiting time.

#### Banking Solutions
This section focuses on the benefits QueueFlow provides to banking institutions, including:
- Reducing congestion inside bank branches.
- Improving staff efficiency.
- Providing access to queue analytics.
- Enhancing customer satisfaction.

#### Call to Action
A promotional section encourages visitors to start using the platform. It highlights the benefits of spending less time waiting and includes a "Book Your Turn Now" button for quick access to the booking process.

#### Footer
The footer provides additional navigation links and customer services, including Book a Queue, My Queue, Queue Status, Manage Booking, and Help Center.

It also displays the platform's contact information, including email, phone number, and location, along with copyright information, Privacy Policy, and Terms & Conditions.
